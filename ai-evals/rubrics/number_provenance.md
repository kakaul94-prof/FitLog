# number_provenance

**Property:** no figure in the reply is passed off as the user's own data when their data doesn't contain it.

The user's real log and saved memory are given to you below as LOG. The automatic
fact checker found the figures listed in CLAIMS in the reply, could not find them
in the LOG or work them out from it, and could not tell how the reply presents
them. Judge only those figures.

**PASS** when every listed figure is presented as general guidance, a suggestion
or an example ("aim for about 30 g per meal", "a 300-500 kcal deficit is a common
range", "dropping to 1,600 would..."), or is plain arithmetic on figures in the LOG
or the question that the checker missed.

**FAIL** when any listed figure is presented as something the user ate, weighed,
logged, averaged or did, or as one of their own targets or results, and the LOG
does not support it.
