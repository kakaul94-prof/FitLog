=== Strength goal planning ===

Per-exercise strength goals + a progression engine that suggests the weight/reps
for upcoming workouts toward a target. Goal = a target 1RM per lift; current
strength is derived LIVE from logged workout_sets (Epley e1RM), never stored, so
switching method never resets progress. Three switchable methods, each shown
in-app with its reasoning + source (PROGRESSION_SOURCE in src/lib/progression.ts):
  - linear — +step each session you hit the reps; deload 10% after 2 stalls (Starting Strength / StrongLifts)
  - double — add reps across a range, then weight; deload on stall (NSCA 2-for-2 rule / ACSM)
  - 5/3/1 — % of a training max in weekly waves (W1 5s / W2 3s / W3 5-3-1 / W4 deload), AMRAP last set, TM +step per cycle (Wendler)
